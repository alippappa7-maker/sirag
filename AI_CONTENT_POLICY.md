# AI Content Policy (سراج)

## 1. Introduction
Siraj uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) as a production assistant to generate ideas, scripts, images, audio, and video structure. This policy governs how AI content is generated, labeled, reviewed, and managed within the application to ensure compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and Islamic guidelines.

## 2. Core Principles
- **AI is an Assistant, Not a Scholar:** AI cannot issue Fatwas, authenticate Hadiths, or deduce legal rulings.
- **Transparency:** All AI-generated assets must carry an explicit disclosure (`generatedByAI = true`).
- **No Impersonation:** Voice cloning or deepfakes of real individuals without explicit permission are strictly prohibited.
- **Respect for Islamic Sanctities:** AI must not be used to depict Prophets, Companions, or Angels.

## 3. Metadata & Tagging
Every asset generated by AI must include `AiMetadata`:
- `generatedByAI`: Boolean flag indicating AI involvement.
- `provider`: AI service provider (e.g., Google Vertex AI, OpenAI).
- `model`: Specific model version (e.g., gemini-1.5-pro).
- `disclosureRequired`: Flag to force UI labeling.
- `safetyStatus`: Tracks if the content was flagged, reviewed, or safe.

## 4. UI/UX Requirements
- **Labeling:** An "AI Generated" (مُوَلَّد بالذكاء الاصطناعي) assist chip must be visible on the content.
- **Disclosure Screen:** Tapping the label opens a bottom sheet explaining that the content was generated by AI and listing any relevant disclaimers.
- **In-App Reporting:** The disclosure screen must contain a prominent "Report" button allowing users to flag abusive, misleading, or inappropriate AI content, complying with App Store and Google Play guidelines.

## 5. Dangerous Prompts & Validation
- Prompts are filtered client-side and server-side to prevent malicious, inappropriate, or religiously offensive content generation.
- Real-person impersonation checks are enforced for audio generation.

## 6. Provider Management
- In emergency cases (e.g., a model hallucinating dangerously), the platform can remotely disable a specific model or provider via `AiProviderConfig`.

## 7. Reporting & Review
- AI content reported by users goes into `PENDING_REVIEW`.
- High-risk topics generated by AI require human review before they can be published (`humanReviewed` flag).
